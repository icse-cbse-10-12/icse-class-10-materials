How to Use the ICSE MasterClass Study Vault to Find Notes, PYQs and Question Banks

The Study Vault is a searchable directory. It helps you discover listed resources; it does not mean every file is hosted by ICSE MasterClass or that every third-party resource is free.

By Team MasterClassPublished: 11 November 2026Updated: 11 November 2026Reading time: 3 minutes

1. Start with the subject directory

Open the ICSE Class 10 Study Vault and choose the subject you need. The directory helps you browse the resources listed for each subject.

2. Search using specific words

Search by a chapter name, topic, resource title or publisher. A specific phrase usually narrows results more effectively than a broad search such as “notes”. If you do not find a result, try a shorter topic name or common alternative spelling.

3. Check details before opening a resource

  • Confirm that the subject and chapter match your current syllabus.
  • Check the publication year, edition and paper format where shown.
  • Read the title and category rather than assuming a result is the right file.
  • Follow the link to the original host and review its terms and access requirements.

4. Understand what the catalogue does

The website displays resource information from its published catalogue. Many links lead to third-party websites or publishers. Those providers control their own content, availability, login requirements and access conditions. A listing does not transfer ownership or grant permission to redistribute a resource.

5. What if a link is missing or broken?

Check the Telegram community for an existing resource request before posting a new one. If you cannot locate the material, send the team the subject, chapter and resource name through Telegram. For copyright or takedown concerns, email icsemasterclass@gmail.com with the exact page or resource URL.

Remember: ICSE MasterClass is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with, authorised by or endorsed by CISCE. Official syllabus and examination information should be checked on the CISCE website.
