ICSE Class 10 Date Sheet 2027: Key Dates and How to Check the Official Timetable

CISCE has announced the 2027 ICSE examination schedule. Use this summary to orient revision, but use the official timetable for the complete subject list, paper timings and instructions.

By Team MasterClassPublished: 11 November 2026Updated: 11 November 2026Reading time: 4 minutes
Quick answer: ICSE Class 10 examinations for 2027 are scheduled from 15 February to 31 March 2027. The timetable was reported as released on 10 November 2026. Verify your paper and timings in the official CISCE document.

This is a selected list, not the complete date sheet. Some Art and elective papers have different start times from the common 10:15 a.m. papers. Read the time printed against your own subject and paper code.

How to verify the official timetable

  1. Open the official CISCE website.
  2. Find the ICSE Year 2027 examination timetable or date-sheet notice.
  3. Check the subject, paper number, date, day, duration and start time.
  4. Compare it with the instructions issued by your school.
  5. Recheck the official notice if CISCE publishes a correction.

Use the date sheet to plan revision

  • List your own subjects in exam order; do not plan from another student's elective combination.
  • Mark the gaps between papers and assign revision tasks before each gap begins.
  • Keep written practice and numerical problem-solving in your weekly routine.
  • Use specimen papers and previous-year questions to understand formats, not to predict the exact paper.
  • Use the final days before a paper for targeted recall and error review.

Quick questions

When do ICSE Class 10 exams begin in 2027?

The announced exam window begins on 15 February 2027 and ends on 31 March 2027. Verify the date for each paper in the official timetable.

When is ICSE Mathematics 2027?

Mathematics is scheduled for 22 February 2027. Check the official PDF for the exact time and instructions.

Are these all subject dates?

No. This is a selected list. Use the complete official CISCE timetable for all subjects, electives and paper timings.
