About the Study Vault

Is ICSE MasterClass an official CISCE website?

No. ICSE MasterClass is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with, authorised by or endorsed by CISCE. Use cisce.org for official syllabus, regulations and examination notices.

Are the resources in the Study Vault free?

The Study Vault is free to browse. Some links lead to third-party publishers or websites that may require an account, payment or permission. Check the original provider's terms before accessing a resource.

Does ICSE MasterClass host every PDF or book listed?

No. The Study Vault is a resource directory and many listings link to third-party hosts. The original provider controls availability and access. A listing does not mean the website owns or grants permission to redistribute that material.

Are the PDFs and notes produced by Team MasterClass?

No. The PDFs, notes, question papers and other downloadable study files listed in the catalogue are not produced or authored by Team MasterClass. The catalogue aggregates or references educational resources found across online sources to make discovery easier. Original authors, publishers and rights holders retain their respective rights. A listing does not grant permission to copy or redistribute a file; check the original source and its terms. For copyright concerns, contact icsemasterclass@gmail.com.

Finding study materials

How do I find notes or question banks for a chapter?

Choose the subject and use the search field with a chapter name, topic, title or publisher. Try a shorter phrase if the first search returns no results. You can also use the available category filters.

What types of study resources can I find?

Depending on the current catalogue, you may find notes, question banks, previous-year questions, specimen or sample papers, reference material and video lessons. Availability varies by subject and source.

Where should I check the ICSE Class 10 syllabus for 2027?

Use the official CISCE ICSE 2027 regulations and syllabus. Confirm the subject and syllabus year before using older study material.

Where can I verify the ICSE Class 10 2027 date sheet?

Check the official CISCE website and use the complete timetable PDF or latest notice. Our independent date-sheet summary is for quick orientation; the official CISCE document takes priority.

Support and corrections

What should I do if a resource link is broken or missing?

Send the subject, chapter and resource name through the ICSE MasterClass Telegram community. Include the exact URL if reporting a broken link.

How can I report a copyright or takedown concern?

Email icsemasterclass@gmail.com with the exact resource or page URL, the material involved and the reason for the request. The existing copyright and disclaimer panel on the Study Vault remains the primary site notice.
