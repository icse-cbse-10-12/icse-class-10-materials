Cookie and Browser Storage Policy

This policy explains how the Study Vault uses browser storage, offline cache and consent preferences. The site does not currently run an analytics SDK.

Your choice matters. Use the persistent “Privacy settings” control on any page to review or change optional preferences. Rejecting optional categories does not block the public resource catalogue or necessary sign-in functions.

1. Necessary storage

  • Firebase Authentication persistence: supports a signed-in session when you choose to sign in.
  • Local study data: the dashboard and study tools store tasks, syllabus progress and session records in your browser. These are browser storage entries, not tracking cookies.
  • Service-worker cache: stores selected site files for offline access. It is application cache, not an advertising cookie.
  • Consent preference: the site stores your versioned category choices and update timestamp in localStorage under icseMasterClassConsentV1.

2. Optional analytics

Analytics is an available preference category, but no analytics SDK is currently activated by the site. The preference is stored so a future analytics feature can remain disabled until you opt in. This setting does not block third-party hosting or all network requests.

3. Optional external services

EduBot is an optional AI feature. It loads Firebase AI Logic only after you enable “Optional external services” in Privacy settings and then interact with EduBot. When used, the question and recent conversation context are sent to Google's Gemini service. Rejecting this category leaves the catalogue, ordinary pages and sign-in available but prevents EduBot from loading.

4. Core third-party requests

The public catalogue retrieves its published resource data from Google Sheets. The site also loads core presentation assets and uses Firebase Authentication when you choose to sign in. These requests support the core service or a feature you explicitly invoke; they are not controlled by the optional analytics switch. Google Sheets catalogue data remains read-only from the visitor website. External YouTube and Telegram destinations load only when you choose to open those links.

5. Changing or clearing choices

Choose “Privacy settings” at the bottom-left of a page to reopen the preference panel. You can accept all optional categories, reject them, or save a custom choice. You can also clear local site data through your browser settings, but doing so may sign you out, erase local study data and remove offline files. Clearing browser data does not delete a Firebase Authentication account.
