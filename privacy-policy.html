Privacy and data use

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · Published by Team MasterClass

This page explains what information the Study Vault processes, where it is stored, which service providers are involved and what choices are available to students and parents.

Important for students and parents: This website is designed for school-age learners. The current sign-up flow does not include built-in age verification or a verifiable parent/guardian-consent process. A parent or guardian should review this notice and supervise use. Do not create an account or submit a child's personal information unless any consent required by applicable law has first been obtained. This is an implementation gap that must be addressed before the service is represented as fully compliant for independent use by children.

1. Who operates this website

The ICSE MasterClass Study Vault is published and operated under the name Team MasterClass. This is the publisher name used for the project and is not a representation that Team MasterClass is a separately incorporated company. For privacy questions or requests, email icsemasterclass@gmail.com.

Geographic audience: The Study Vault is primarily intended for students in India who use or study the Indian Certificate of Secondary Education (ICSE) curriculum, along with their parents and educators. Our website's intended user community is Indian; we do not represent the service as being specifically targeted at students or schools in the United Arab Emirates, Oman, the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Indonesia or other overseas markets. The Study Vault is an independent educational resource project and is not operated, endorsed or affiliated with the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE).

The website may technically be reachable from outside India, but accessibility from another country does not mean that country is part of our intended user market. We will assess privacy rights and legal requirements based on the actual circumstances of the service, its users and its data processing, rather than assuming that a law applies or does not apply solely from a visitor's nationality or location. We do not use the optional self-selected region preference to infer a visitor's location or to switch off privacy protections for any region.

2. Information processed

  • Account and sign-in information: When you create an account or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes the email address and authentication identifiers needed to manage the account. Google sign-in may also provide profile information such as your name and email address. The password is submitted to Firebase Authentication for account creation or sign-in and is not written to the website's own database or browser-local study storage by this application.
  • Study information stored in your browser: The student dashboard and study tools save tasks, syllabus progress, study-session records and tool preferences in browser storage on the device you use. The dashboard code stores this information locally and does not provide an automatic server-sync feature. Clearing site data or changing browsers/devices may remove access to these records. Export a backup where the feature is available.
  • EduBot messages: When you use EduBot after enabling optional external services, the question you submit and recent conversation context (up to 10 messages held in the page's memory, including user and assistant messages) are sent to the EduBot API hosted on Vercel. The API uses Google Gemini as its primary provider and may use Groq as a fallback when Gemini is unavailable. The Study Vault does not intentionally save the chat transcript to its own database or browser storage. Vercel and the AI provider that handles a request may process request content and technical information under their own terms, privacy notices and retention practices; we cannot guarantee their retention or model-use practices. Do not enter passwords, phone numbers, addresses, school identifiers or other private information into EduBot.
  • Offline website data: The service worker stores selected website files in your browser cache so pages can load offline. This includes application files and the published legal pages. Browser cache data remains on your device until cleared or replaced by the browser/service worker.
  • Public resource catalogue: The materials catalogue reads published spreadsheet data from Google Sheets. The catalogue is intended to be read-only for visitors. Google may receive standard request information when the browser retrieves that data.
  • Restricted administration: The website contains an administrator interface that can connect to Supabase for administrator sign-in and management of study-resource records and files. This is an administrative feature, not the student dashboard's progress store. Supabase processes information according to the configuration and policies of the relevant project.
  • Technical request information: The site is hosted through GitHub Pages and loads some third-party assets, including Tailwind CSS and Font Awesome CDN resources. Those providers and the hosting provider may receive standard technical information such as IP address, browser details and request logs when your browser connects to them.

3. Why information is used

Information is processed to provide account sign-in, display study resources, remember study activity on your device, support offline access, respond to EduBot questions, maintain the website and protect administrative functions. We do not use browser-local study progress to create a server-side student profile in the current implementation.

4. Third-party services and external links

The website uses Google Sheets for the published resource catalogue, GitHub Pages for hosting, and a Vercel-hosted EduBot API that may route study questions to Google Gemini or Groq. Firebase Authentication is optional and its SDK is loaded only after you enable Optional external services and choose an account action. Supabase is restricted to administrator workflows and is also consent-gated. The catalogue and dashboard currently request Tailwind CSS and Font Awesome presentation assets from public CDNs; these are core layout/icon dependencies, not analytics or session replay, and the CDN providers may receive request metadata such as an IP address. Google Fonts are not requested. The 404 page uses a native system font stack and no external script. External YouTube and Telegram destinations load only when you choose to open those links. Each provider's own privacy notice and terms apply.

5. Cookies, browser storage and offline cache

Student study features are designed to work without an account. Saved materials, syllabus progress, tasks, study-session records, Study Tools preferences and theme settings are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. These records are not automatically synced to our servers and are not available on another browser or device unless you export and move a backup. Browser-local records may still contain personal information if you type identifying details into task names or notes; avoid doing so. Clearing site data may erase these records. The service worker separately uses the browser Cache API for offline website files.

Firebase Authentication is optional and is loaded only after you enable Optional external services in Privacy settings and choose to sign in or create an account. If a user signs in, Firebase Authentication may persist that account session in browser storage; this is not used by guest mode. The privacy-preference manager stores a versioned choice and timestamp in localStorage under icseMasterclassConsentV1. The administrator page stores its Supabase project URL and public/publishable key in that browser's localStorage. The in-product preference manager controls optional analytics and external services; it does not erase data held by third-party providers.

The Cookie Policy explains these storage categories, the absence of an active analytics or session-replay SDK, and the consent gate for Firebase Authentication, EduBot and administrator Supabase.

6. Retention

Browser-local study records remain until you clear them or the browser removes them. Cached files remain until cleared or replaced. EduBot conversation context exists in the active page session and is cleared when that page context is discarded; requests already sent to Vercel and the relevant AI provider are subject to their respective terms and retention practices. Firebase account information is retained by Firebase while the account exists and may remain in provider backups or logs for periods governed by the provider. Contact us if you want help with a request to delete an account; deletion may require action in the relevant provider console.

7. Children and parent/guardian consent

The DPDP Act defines a child as a person who has not completed 18 years of age. Section 9 provides for verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian before processing a child's personal data and restricts processing likely to harm a child's well-being, tracking or behavioural monitoring of children, and targeted advertising directed at children, subject to statutory exceptions. Under the commencement notifications G.S.R. 843(E) and G.S.R. 846(E), section 9 and Rules 3 and 5–16 (including Rule 10) are scheduled to commence 18 months after publication, on 13 May 2027. This website is adopting conservative controls ahead of that date.

The site does not currently verify a visitor's age or a parent's identity and age. The notice on the sign-in page is not a verification mechanism and does not itself constitute verifiable parental consent. Until a suitable verification process is implemented, students under 18 should use guest access only for local study features; they should not create an account, sign in, submit personal details or use EduBot or other third-party processing features. Guest study features remain local to the browser. EduBot is not local-only: questions and recent conversation context (up to 10 messages) are sent to the Vercel-hosted API and may be processed by Google Gemini or Groq. Do not include personal information in EduBot prompts.

No analytics or session-replay SDK is currently installed. The site does not intentionally use children's study progress for behavioural advertising or targeted advertising. See the DPDP Act commencement notification (G.S.R. 843(E)) and the DPDP Rules, 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E)).

8. Your choices and requests

You can reopen Privacy settings on any page to change optional categories. You can clear dashboard and Study Tools data for the current browser using the dashboard’s “Clear all local study data” control, or clear site data through your browser settings. Clearing browser data does not delete your Firebase Authentication account. The Firebase account-deletion Cloud Function has source code in this repository but is not yet deployed or verified; until deployment and verification are complete, request Firebase account deletion by contacting icsemasterclass@gmail.com. No online account-deletion success is claimed at this stage. For access, correction or other privacy requests, use the contact details in Section 11. Include enough detail to identify the relevant account, but do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents by ordinary email. We may need to verify that a request is genuine. Requests involving Firebase, Google or Supabase data may also need to be made to the relevant provider.

9. Security

We use the technical controls available in the website and its service providers, but no internet transmission or browser storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a device you control, sign out on shared devices, keep your account credentials private and avoid submitting sensitive information to AI features.

10. Changes and legal framework

This notice may be updated when website features, providers or applicable law change. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 have phased commencement dates. As of 4 November 2026, not all provisions are in force; the official commencement notification schedules many core processing obligations for 13 May 2027, with certain provisions commencing earlier. This notice is an operational disclosure, not a legal opinion or certification of compliance.
