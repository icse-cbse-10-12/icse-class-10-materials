About ICSE MasterClass Study Vault

The Free Educational Index for ICSE Class 10 Board Preparation

Welcome to the digital resource library by ICSE MasterClass. Designed for Class 10 students preparing for the Indian Certificate of Secondary Education examinations, this platform acts as a centralized index for study materials, revision notes, previous-year question papers and publisher question banks.

Our mission is to make educational resources easier to discover. Whether you are revising Physics and Mathematics, practicing Biology diagrams, learning Java programming for Computer Applications, or reviewing English grammar, our vault helps students find relevant learning resources.

Extensive Subject Support

Resources across subjects including Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, History and Civics, Geography, Computer Applications, Commercial Studies, Economic Applications, Environmental Applications, Home Science, Physical Education, English Language, English Literature and Second Languages.

Curated Resource Formats

Discover sample papers, chapter summaries, formula sheets, question banks, reference resources and educational video lectures.
